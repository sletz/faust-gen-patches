GEN~ Audio DSP Library in Faust

The 21 gen~ models and 4 utilities of Max Bruckert's GEN-patches, ported to Faust and checked sample by sample against the C++ that Max exports from each patch. Every card plays its port in the browser: the Faust program compiled to WebAssembly by faust-rs, in double precision, in an AudioWorklet. Open Play on a card to hear it and drive it.
